MAP OF TANZANIA

Map of Tanzania. Map description. Tanzania occupies the centre of the map and is divided into regions by thin grey regional boundaries. A thick black line forms the international boundary. Uganda lies to the north-west. Rwanda and Burundi lie to the west. The Democratic Republic of Congo lies to the west across Lake Tanganyika. Zambia lies to the south-west. Malawi lies beside Lake Nyasa to the south-west. Mozambique lies to the south. Kenya lies to the north-east. The blue Indian Ocean borders Tanzania on the east, with offshore islands shown near the coast. Blue water bodies include Lake Victoria in the north; Lake Natron, Lake Eyasi and Lake Manyara in the north-central area; Lake Tanganyika along the west; Lake Rukwa in the south-western interior; and Lake Nyasa along the south-western boundary. Dodoma is marked near the centre of Tanzania by a small outlined square containing a black dot, identifying the capital city. The longitude markings are thirty, thirty-three, thirty-six and thirty-nine degrees east. The latitude markings are three, six, nine and twelve degrees south. Grey cross marks indicate coordinate-grid intersections. A north arrow in the upper-right points north. Key. A thick black line represents an international boundary. A thin grey outline represents a regional boundary. A small outlined square containing a black dot represents the capital city. Blue represents a water body. Source: National Bureau of Statistics, 2019. Author: Tanzania Institute of Education. Scale. The scale bar has alternating black-and-white sections and is marked five hundred kilometres on the left, zero at the centre, and five hundred kilometres on the right.
