Introduction

This textbook, Geography and the Environment for Primary Schools is written based on the Geography and the Environment syllabus for primary education standards III-VI, issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology in 2023. Some of the contents in this book have been taken from the Social Studies textbooks for Standard III, IV, and V, published in 2018, following the 2016 syllabus. This textbook contains four chapters: The concept of a map, the Cardinal Directions, Drawing a simple map, and The uses of maps. By learning the contents of this textbook, you will develop the knowledge, skills, and attitude necessary for map drawing, reading, and interpretation.

The content of each chapter is presented in the form of text, activities, different maps and diagrams to enable you to develop the intended competencies. Each chapter has sufficient exercises to test your understanding of the specific themes. Therefore, you are advised to do all the exercises and activities in this textbook, along with any additional activities provided by the teacher to develop the intended competencies.

Additional learning resources are available in the TIE library, at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
