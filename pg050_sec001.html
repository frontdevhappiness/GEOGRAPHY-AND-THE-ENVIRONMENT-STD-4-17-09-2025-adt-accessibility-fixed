Activity 8

1. Use an atlas or any reliable online sources to find a map of Tanzania, then draw it and present the following features:

(a) Mount Kilimanjaro;
(b) The Indian Ocean;
(c) Lake Victoria;
(d) Lake Tanganyika;
(e) Lake Nyasa; and
(f) The Islands of Pemba, Unguja, and Mafia.

2. Add a key, a North direction, a frame, and a title to the map you have drawn.
