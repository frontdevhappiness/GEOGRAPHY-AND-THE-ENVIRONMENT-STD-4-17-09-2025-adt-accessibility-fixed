Tanzania Regional Administrative Map

Tanzania Regional Administrative Map. Map description. Tanzania’s regions are displayed in different colours and separated by thin grey regional boundaries. A thick black outer line marks international boundaries. Uganda lies to the north-west; Rwanda, Burundi and the Democratic Republic of Congo lie to the west; Zambia and Malawi lie to the south-west; Mozambique lies to the south; Kenya lies to the north-east; and the Indian Ocean lies to the east. Blue water bodies include Lake Victoria in the north, Lake Tanganyika in the west, Lake Rukwa in the south-west, Lake Nyasa along the south-western border, Lakes Natron, Manyara and Eyasi in the north, and the Indian Ocean. Northern and north-western regions include Kagera, Geita, Mwanza, Mara, Simiyu, Shinyanga, Arusha, Kilimanjaro and Manyara. Western and central regions include Kigoma, Katavi, Rukwa, Tabora, Singida and Dodoma. Southern regions include Songwe, Mbeya, Iringa, Njombe, Ruvuma, Lindi and Mtwara. Eastern regions include Tanga, Morogoro, Pwani and Dar es Salaam. The islands present Kaskazini Pemba, Kusini Pemba, Kaskazini Unguja and Kusini Unguja. The colours distinguish neighbouring regions. Red regions include Geita, Arusha, Iringa and Dar es Salaam. Orange regions include Kagera, Kigoma, Katavi and Kilimanjaro. Yellow regions include Mara, Manyara, Dodoma, Mbeya and Lindi. Green regions include Mwanza, Shinyanga, Rukwa, Morogoro, Pwani, Njombe, Ruvuma and Mtwara. Blue or blue-green regions include Simiyu, Tabora, Singida, Tanga and Songwe. The island regions use red, orange, yellow and grey. Repeated colours do not represent a shared category; they help distinguish adjacent regions. A north arrow points north. Key. A thin grey line represents a regional boundary. A thick black line represents an international boundary. Blue represents a water body. Source: National Bureau of Statistics, 2019. Author: Tanzania Institute of Education. Scale. The scale bar is marked five hundred on the left, zero at the centre, and five hundred kilometres on the right.

Road and railway maps

These maps provide important information about road and railway networks, such as highways and street roads, and important signs or symbols of a certain area as presented in Figure 3. They also present the distance between different locations and often provide information about petrol stations, restaurants, and hotels. Road and railway maps are important for drivers and travellers.
