Activity 13:

Use Google My Maps software

  1. 1. Use a smartphone, tablet or computer to open your browser, and type Google My Maps.
  2. 2. Using the Google My Maps interface, click on the search section.
  3. 3. Type the name of your district in the search section, then click search.
  4. 4. After that, find the number of primary schools available in your district by typing, for example, “primary schools in Chamwino District”; and
  5. 5. Describe the distribution of primary schools in your district.
