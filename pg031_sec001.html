Steps to determine Cardinal Directions using a digital compass

(a) take your compass or download the compass App;

(b) click the App and and make sure the compass direction arrow is pointing the North (N);

(c) when the compass direction arrow is pointing North, orient yourself to that direction, this help you to detarmine the North direction of your location; and

(d) once you identify the North direction, you can determine the other directions. For example, the South will be at your back, the East will be on your right, and the West will be on your left.

The intercardinal directions

The intercardinal directions are points that lie between the Cardinal Directions. They provide for more specific directional guidance to basic Cardinal Directions. These directions are Northeast (NE), Southeast (SE), Southwest (SW), and Northwest (NW). They offer more detailed explanations or specific directions, leading to a more precise and accurate understanding of a location. Intercardinal directions are commonly used in various fields, such as aviation, maritime navigation, geography, and weather forecasting.
