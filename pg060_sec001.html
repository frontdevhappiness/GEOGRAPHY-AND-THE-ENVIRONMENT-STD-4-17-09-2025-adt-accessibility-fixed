The uses of map
Introduction

In life, sometimes we need to travel from one place to another that we are not familiar with, to access services, relax, or visit relatives and friends. It can be difficult to reach the intended place if we do not have someone to guide us. However, a map is a useful tool that can help you to find the route and guide you to your destination. In this chapter, you will learn how to use a map to find directions, measure distance, identify administrative boundaries, and determine relief of the Earth’s surface. You will also learn how to use maps to determine the population of a particular place. The competencies developed will enable you to use maps in different contexts.
