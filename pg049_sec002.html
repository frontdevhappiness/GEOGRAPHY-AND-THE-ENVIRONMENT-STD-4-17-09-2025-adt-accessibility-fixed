Activity 7

Use an atlas or any reliable online sources to find a map of Tanzania, that shows administrative boundaries. Then, identify the region where you live and do the following:

  • (a) Draw a map of the region where you live;
  • (b) Show the districts of that region;
  • (c) Shade the district where you live;
  • (d) Place a North arrow to show direction; and
  • (e) Add a map key.
