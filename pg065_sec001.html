(c) A map distance from point A to B marked on the folded paper can be converted into real ground distance using a linear scale or ratio scale.

To use a linear scale, place the folded paper along the scale by aligning point A with zero (0) and point B with the corresponding point on the right side of the linear scale.

If point B is aligned with a certain number on the scale, as presented in Figure 6, that number represents the real ground distance between points A and B.

In this case, the ground distance from point A to B is 3 kilometres.

Figure 6: Point B is aligned with the number 3, representing a distance of 3 kilometres from point A to point B A linear scale is positioned above a pale-yellow folded paper strip. Point A aligns directly below zero. Point B aligns directly below three kilometres, representing a distance of three kilometres.
