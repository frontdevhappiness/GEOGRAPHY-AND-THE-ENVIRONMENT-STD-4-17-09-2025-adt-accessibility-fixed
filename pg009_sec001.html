Topographical Map of Tanga Region

Topographical Map of Tanga Region. Map description. Tanga Region occupies the centre of the map. Kenya lies beyond the thick black international boundary to the north. Kilimanjaro is to the north-west, Manyara to the west, Morogoro to the south-west, and Pwani to the south. The blue Indian Ocean borders Tanga on the east. Kaskazini Unguja and Mjini Magharibi lie across the ocean to the south-east. Red roads form an interconnected network throughout Tanga, with routes crossing the northern and central areas and extending towards the western, southern and coastal boundaries. Light-blue rivers cross the interior. The Pangani River follows western and central Tanga before flowing towards the Indian Ocean. Thin brown contour lines cover much of the region, particularly the northern, central and western terrain. Green forest patches are scattered through the northern, central and coastal areas. A north arrow points north. Key. A red line represents a road. A light-blue line represents a river. A thin brown line represents a contour. A green block represents forest. A blue block represents ocean. A grey line represents a regional boundary. A thick black line represents an international boundary. Scale. The scale bar is marked one hundred on the left, zero at the centre, and one hundred kilometres on the right. Source: National Bureau of Statistics, 2019, USGS, RCMRD and ESRI. Author: Tanzania Institute of Education.

Political maps

These maps provide information about the international, regional, district and other local boundaries. They also present important areas such as government buildings, capital cities, and towns, as presented in Figure 2. Political maps are helpful for researchers, learners, and visitors who need to understand the administrative boundaries of a certain area.

Political maps change over time. Several factors can cause changes in a political map. These include wars, boundaries conflicts between countries, the nationalism, political agreements, and a boundary change within a country. Because of these factors, political maps are updated to present the current national boundaries or other specific areas.
