Using a map to identify administrative boundaries

Activity 7

Use online reliable sources or books to study the map of Tanzania and identify different administrative boundaries.

✎
The following are important aspects to consider to identify administrative boundaries correctly:
(a) Selecting the appropriate map;
(b) Identifying a reliable source of information including Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, or special government software; and
(c)
Recognizing the symbols and signs used on the map to show administrative boundaries. Several times the lines of different sizes, shapes or colours used to represent different types of administrative boundaries. Some boundaries are identified by various features, such as rivers, lakes, mountains, and roads.
Generally, identifying administrative boundaries is important for various purposes, including governance, decision-making, the management of public resources and the implementation of policies and development plans.
