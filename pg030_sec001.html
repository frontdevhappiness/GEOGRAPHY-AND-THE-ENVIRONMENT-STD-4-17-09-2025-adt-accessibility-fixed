Determining the Cardinal Directions through a compass

A compass is a device used to identify the North direction. There are two types of compasses, namely: digital compass and analog compass. A digital compass uses technology to detect and display the North direction. This type of compass is often integrated into GPS, modern watches, smartphones, and transport vehicles such as cars, boats, and planes, as shown in Figure 4. An analog compass uses a magnetized needle to point to the North.
