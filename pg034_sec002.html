The importance of Cardinal Directions

The Cardinal Directions are very important for daily human life. The following are the importance of the cardinal direction.

(a) It helps to identify directions and places on maps and the earth’s surface. This makes it easier for users to identify their present location and where they are heading. They also help to direct people to reach their destination easily;

(b) it is useful to engineers and architects in designing buildings, roads, and railways. Understanding Cardinal Directions is crucial for determining the orientation of a construction site, taking into account factors like the direction of the sunrise and sunset.
