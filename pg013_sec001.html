Rainfall Distribution in Iringa Region. Map description. Iringa Region occupies the centre of the map. Singida lies to the north-west, Dodoma to the north-east, Morogoro to the east, Njombe to the south, and Mbeya to the west. A north arrow points north. Rainfall generally increases from north to south. Iringa District Council covers most of the north and is mainly very pale blue, representing very low rainfall. Its southern edge changes to light blue, representing low rainfall. Iringa Municipal Council is a small central area with very low rainfall. Kilolo District Council in the east has very low rainfall in the north and low to moderate rainfall towards the south. A light-blue to medium-blue band crosses the central part of the region, representing low and moderate rainfall. Mafinga Town Council and much of Mufindi District Council in the south are deeper blue, representing high rainfall. The darkest navy-blue areas occur in southern Mufindi near the Njombe border, representing very high rainfall. Key. A thick grey line represents a regional boundary. A thin grey line represents a district boundary. Very pale blue represents very low rainfall. Light blue represents low rainfall. Medium blue represents moderate rainfall. Deep blue represents high rainfall. Navy blue represents very high rainfall. Source: CHIRPS, 2023. Author: Tanzania Institute of Education. Scale. The scale bar is marked one hundred on the left, zero, one hundred, and two hundred kilometres on the right.
