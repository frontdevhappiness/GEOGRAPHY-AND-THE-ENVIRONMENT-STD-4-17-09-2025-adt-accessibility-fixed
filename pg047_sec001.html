Figure 5: A map of Sinza WardThe ward occupies the central area on a pale background. Its neighbouring areas are Makongo to the north, Ubungo to the west, Kijitonyama to the east and Manzese to the south-east. Thick grey irregular lines mark ward boundaries. The blue Ngombe River curves along the southern boundary from west to east. Red roads cross the ward. Sam Nujoma Road runs diagonally from the south-west towards the north. Mori Road begins near the northern junction and curves south-east. Shekilango Road crosses the central area towards the east. Mlandizi Road runs through the lower-central area towards the south-east. A north arrow in the upper-right points north. Two magenta squares represent public offices, including the labelled Ward Office in the lower-central area. Two outlined S C symbols represent schools. Three outlined D S symbols represent dispensaries. Four outlined H symbols represent hospitals. Four cross-and-building symbols represent churches. Three crescent-and-star symbols represent mosques. Key. A magenta square means public office; S C means school; D S means dispensary; H means hospital; a cross above a building means church; a crescent and star mean mosque; a thin blue line means river; a thin red line means road; and a thick grey line means ward boundary. No scale, source or author is displayed. A pale-red diagonal online-reading watermark crosses the map.
