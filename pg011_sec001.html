Tanzania Roads and Railways Network Map

Tanzania Roads and Railways Network Map. Map description. Tanzania occupies the centre of the map. Uganda lies to the north-west; Rwanda, Burundi and the Democratic Republic of Congo lie to the west; Zambia and Malawi lie to the south-west; Mozambique lies to the south; Kenya lies to the north-east; and the Indian Ocean lies to the east. Red roads form a network across Tanzania. In the north, roads connect Kagera, Geita, Mwanza, Mara, Simiyu, Shinyanga, Arusha and Kilimanjaro. Central roads connect Tabora, Singida and Dodoma. Eastern roads connect Tanga, Morogoro, Pwani and Dar es Salaam. Western and southern roads extend through Kigoma, Katavi, Rukwa, Songwe, Mbeya, Iringa, Njombe, Ruvuma, Lindi and Mtwara. Black railway lines with short crossbars include an east-to-west route from Dar es Salaam through Morogoro, Dodoma and Tabora towards Kigoma. Other railway branches extend north towards Mwanza, north-east towards Tanga and Kilimanjaro, and south-west through Iringa, Mbeya and Songwe towards Zambia. Blue water bodies include Lakes Victoria, Tanganyika, Rukwa, Nyasa, Natron, Eyasi and Manyara, together with the Indian Ocean. Zanzibar’s islands lie off the eastern coast. A north arrow points north. Key. A thin grey line represents a regional boundary. A thick black line represents an international boundary. A red line represents a road. A black line with short crossbars represents a railway. Blue represents water. Source: National Bureau of Statistics, 2019, and ICPAC. Author: Tanzania Institute of Education. Scale. The scale bar is marked five hundred on the left, zero at the centre, and five hundred kilometres on the right.
