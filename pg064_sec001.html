You can determine the distance from one place to another on a map by following these steps:

  • (a) Measure the distance on the map between the two places; and
  • (b) Convert the map distance into the ground distance using the scale.

This measurement can include both straight and curved features and can be done with both digital and non-digital maps.

Measurement of straight distance

On a map, straight distance is measured using a piece of paper, thread or string, a ruler, a pair of dividers, and a scale. In this section, a piece of paper, a ruler, and a scale will be used.
