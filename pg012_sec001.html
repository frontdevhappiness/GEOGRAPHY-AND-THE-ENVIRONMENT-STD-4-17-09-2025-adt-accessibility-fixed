Thematic maps

These maps represent specific and unique themes or information. Thematic maps can vary depending on the theme being presented. For example, thematic maps can represent types of soils, vegetation, crops, human populations, weather, and urban planning.

Population map

This map represents the density or distribution of people in a certain area. Population maps help us to know where areas are crowded or not crowded. They are useful for people who make decisions about where to put important things like schools, hospitals, markets, and roads. Figure 4 presents a map of population distribution in Tabora Region.

Tabora Population Distribution Map. Map description. Tabora Region occupies the centre of the map. It is bordered by Geita to the north-west, Shinyanga to the north, Simiyu to the north-east, Singida to the east, Mbeya and Songwe to the south, Rukwa and Katavi to the south-west, and Kigoma to the west. Lake Sagara is shown in blue along the western side. A north arrow points north. The district colours represent population size. Kaliua District Council in the west is dark red, representing a very large number of people. Nzega District Council in the north, Igunga District Council in the north-east, and Uyui District Council in the east are red-orange, representing a large number of people. Tabora Municipal Council in the centre and Sikonge District Council in the south are orange, representing a moderate number of people. Urambo District Council in the west-central area is pale yellow, representing a small number of people. Nzega Town Council is pale cream, representing a very small number of people. Key. A thin grey line represents a district boundary. A thicker grey line represents a regional boundary. Blue represents water. Pale cream represents a very small number of people. Pale yellow represents a small number. Orange represents a moderate number. Red-orange represents a large number. Dark red represents a very large number. Source: National Bureau of Statistics, 2022. Author: Tanzania Institute of Education. Scale. The scale bar is marked one hundred on the left, zero, one hundred, and two hundred kilometres on the right.
