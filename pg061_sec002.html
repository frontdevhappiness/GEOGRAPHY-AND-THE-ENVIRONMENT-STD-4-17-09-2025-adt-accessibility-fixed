Geography and the Environment Pupil's Book Standard 4

The direction and location of features on a map can be identified using the symbol of the North direction. The North direction presents the orientation of North, South, East, and West. This will help you determine the location of different features on the earth’s surface. It will also help you to find the directions from one place to another.

Similarly, we can find directions and locations using digital maps and GPS. GPS helps you determine where you are, while a digital map presents information about where you want to go. After determining your present location and identifying your destination, the GPS calculates and presents the distance and direction to follow. Digital maps and GPS are found on electronic devices such as smartphones, tablets, and computers. Different groups of people, including ship captains, drivers, pilots, geologists, and land surveyors, use maps in their work to determine locations, distances, and directions.
